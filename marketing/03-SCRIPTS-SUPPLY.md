**Phase:** 1 — ship immediately. These do **not** require live guest inventory.
**Primary CTA:** `hamlet.rest/partners/apply`
**Total: 6 scripts** (S1 hosts ×3 · S2 closing agents ×2 · S3 diaspora landlord ×1)

## ⚠️ PRICING REALITY CHECK — read before shooting S1-A or S1-C

The "0% owner fee" claim is true and is the strongest supply-side message HAMLET has. But it must not be extended into a claim the numbers don't support.

Under Airbnb's common **split-fee** configuration in Nigeria, the host service fee is roughly **3%** of the booking subtotal and the guest service fee roughly **14%** (Airbnb's *host-only / simplified* pricing instead charges the host ~15–16% and the guest nothing extra). Exact rates depend on the host's own account configuration.

| | Host pays | Guest pays on top of host rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Airbnb (split fee, common NG config)** | ~3% | ~14% |
| **Airbnb (host-only / simplified)** | ~15–16% | ~0% |
| **HAMLET** | **0%** | **20%** (+10% FX buffer on $/£ quotes) |

**Therefore:**

- ✅ **Safe and true:** *"You keep 100% of your listed rate. We take nothing from you."*
- ✅ **Safe and true:** *"Our fee sits on the guest side, and we show it to them before they pay."*
- ✅ **Safe and true:** *"You pay 0%, and you get a paid agent network bringing you bookings."*
- ❌ **Never say:** *"Cheaper than Airbnb"* or *"Lower fees than the other platforms."* **On the guest side, 20% is higher than Airbnb's ~14%.** A Nigerian commenter will do this maths within minutes and the campaign will lose.
- ❌ **Never show a side-by-side total-cost comparison** unless the owner has approved the exact competitor figures and legal has reviewed them.

The honest, and still very strong, host pitch is: **"Your rate is yours. All of it. We earn on the guest side, we disclose it, and we bring you distribution you'd otherwise pay an informal agent for."**

> **Strategic recommendation (repeat of `00` §4 Decision 1):** at 20%, HAMLET is the most expensive rail for a Nigerian guest among the majors, in the exact season the market revolted against price. Consider a **pilot guest rate of 5–10%** for the first 90 days, marketed as launch pricing. It converts the biggest liability into a scarcity story and makes every guest-side script dramatically easier to write. **This is an owner decision; nothing below assumes it has been made** — the scripts are written to work at 20%, and each notes where it would strengthen at a lower rate.

It also pre-empts the comment-section attack that would otherwise define the campaign.

- Do not compare to Airbnb or Booking.com totals. See the pricing reality check at the top of this file.
- Do not run paid amplification on this until comment sentiment has been reviewed for 72 hours. It is designed to attract argument — that's the point, but it must be staffed.
