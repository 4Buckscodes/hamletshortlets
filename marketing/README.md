## The argument in six lines

1. **Lagos does not need another shortlet app.** It has 8,691 active listings averaging **38% occupancy** and **$4,500/year**. The market is supply-saturated and the median host is losing.
2. **So the wedge is `0% owner fee`.** The owner keeps 100% of their rate. No incumbent can copy that without breaking their P&L.
3. **And the agent is a channel, not a competitor.** Lagos bookings already run through agents. HAMLET is the only platform that **pays them — 10% of B, recorded, zero inventory required.** That is a self-recruiting salesforce.
4. **Trust, not attention, is the scarce asset.** Nigerians booked through agents rather than platforms *"which speaks to trust concerns"* (MO Africa). Every script therefore carries a **proof mechanism**, not an attention mechanism.
5. **The 20% markup is the biggest risk in the campaign.** December 2025 saw ₦700,000/night one-beds in Lekki Phase 1, a Vanguard headline reading *"Nigerians boycott shortlet apartments for hotels,"* and a January occupancy collapse. **Answer the fee on camera before anyone asks it** — `S1-C` and `DI-4` ch.5 exist only for this.
6. **The Dec 2026 diaspora window has already closed** (they book Sept–Nov). Use **Jan–Aug 2027 — the occupancy desert — to recruit supply**, and be unavoidable for **Detty December 2027**.

## The five decisions that block production

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Is the **20% guest markup** final, or does it drop for pilot? | Consider **5–10% pilot pricing** for 90 days. Turns the biggest liability into a scarcity story. Note: at 20%, HAMLET's guest-side fee is **higher than Airbnb's ~14%** — never claim "cheaper." |
| **2** | Retire the dead **"3% service fee"** line in `MVP_SPECIFICATION.md`? | **Yes.** One-line fix, removes a misrepresentation risk. |
| **3** | What is the **live-inventory gate** for Phase 2? | **15 approved stays, ≥3 areas, ≥2 Mainland.** Concentrated beats the original 50 spread across 16 neighbourhoods. |
| **4** | Real properties or sample sets? | **Real only.** The site rejects stock/sample photos at inspection — video must obey the same rule. |
| **5** | Who is the **on-camera face**? | One consistent human, plus real hosts and real agents. Not a voiceover over B-roll. |

## Non-negotiable compliance rules

The brand *is* honesty, so a marketing claim the product cannot support is the exact behaviour HAMLET exists to punish.

🚫 **Never:** show an unapproved room tour · use the site's sample interiors as real inventory · claim ratings (public reviews are **disabled** in pilot) · claim guaranteed occupancy · claim live availability at 0 listings · quote "3%" · say "escrow" · imply split payments · claim "cheaper than Airbnb."

✅ **Full register with exact approved wording:** `06-EXECUTION-PLAN.md` §7. **If a claim is not in that table with a ✅, it does not ship.**
