# HAMLET guest campaign: hooks, scripts and copy

Goal: get guests to **hamlet.rest** to find and request a verified Lagos stay.
Voice: warm, plain, direct. Sentence case. One idea per line. A little Lagos in the details, never slang for its own sake.

---

## 1. What we know (the analysis)

### Who books
| Audience | Situation | What they fear | What wins them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nigerians abroad and international visitors** | Booking from London, Houston or Toronto, often for December or a family event | Paying a stranger they can't see; the flat not matching the photos; arriving at night with no address | Verified listings, nothing to pay until the host says yes, prices shown in pounds or dollars, the address and arrival guide in their account |
| **Work travellers** | A few nights near VI, Ikoyi, Lekki or Ikeja for meetings | Power, a wasted first morning, surprise charges on the company card | Backup power checked on-site, the total shown up front, a quick request |
| **Lagos residents** | A weekend away without leaving the city | It not being worth the money, or not matching the pictures | Real photos, clear price, a few taps to request |

### The pains we answer (real Lagos shortlet problems)
1. **"Is it even real?"** Fake listings and agents asking for transfers before anyone has seen the place.
2. **"Will it look like the photos?"**
3. **"Is there light?"** Power is the first question every Lagos guest asks.
4. **"What will it actually cost?"** Fees appearing at the end.
5. **"Where exactly am I going?"** Arriving late, gate codes, directions.

### What HAMLET can truthfully say (proof points)
- **Every listing passes four checks before it goes live:** ownership or tenancy rights, permission to let, the person behind the listing, and an on-site visit.
- **On that visit we check the photos, the backup power and the address.**
- **Nothing to pay until the host accepts your request.** You then have 48 hours to pay.
- **The nightly price already includes our service fee. Nothing is added at checkout.** Cleaning and the refundable caution deposit are shown separately, before you request.
- **You pay inside your HAMLET account through Paystack.** Card details are never stored on HAMLET. The stay is confirmed only when the payment is verified.
- **The exact address, arrival guide and door or gate codes unlock in your account once the stay is confirmed.**
- **Booking from abroad?** See prices in US dollars or British pounds. You're charged in naira.
- **Horatio**, HAMLET's assistant, answers questions about areas and stays.
- Areas: Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki Phase 1, Ikate, Oniru, Ajah, Banana Island, Yaba, Surulere, Gbagada, Maryland, Ikeja GRA and more.

Sources: `app/how-it-works/page.tsx`, `app/safety/page.tsx`, `app/v0-home-experience.tsx`, `PRODUCT.md`.

### Do not say
- "Hundreds of stays", "the biggest", "the cheapest" (inventory is still growing).
- Reviews, ratings, star counts or guest quotes (none exist yet).
- "Free cancellation" or any refund promise beyond the caution deposit (no public policy yet).
- "24/7 support", "guaranteed power", "guaranteed water". We *checked* backup power; we don't guarantee supply.
- "Pay in dollars". Prices can be *shown* in USD/GBP; the charge is in naira.

## 7. Production notes
- Every hook works on mute: the first on-screen line *is* the hook.
- Show real HAMLET listing photos only (from Supabase or `public/images`), never stock "luxury" shots that could look like the scams we're calling out.
- One promise per video. Rotate scripts 1, 2 and 3 first: trust, power and December are the strongest pulls this season.
- Track by angle: give each script its own UTM, e.g. `hamlet.rest/?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=guest_trust`.
