# HAMLET Partner Network Registration

Users may join the partner network immediately after creating an active HAMLET account. Membership does not promote a guest profile to the privileged partner role and does not grant property access, private owner data, booking operations or payouts.

Registration captures a primary role (property owner, authorised manager, multi-property operator or closing agent), business/display name and phone. Suspended members cannot self-reactivate. Active administrators can list members and suspend/reactivate them with a reason of at least ten characters; actions are written to the admin audit log.

Property authority, identity checks and listing approval remain separate progressive-trust steps. The existing partner application approval workflow continues to be the only route that grants the privileged `profiles.role='partner'`.

Release the additive migration only after reconciling production migration history, rehearsing against a production-like database, and confirming a backup/rollback plan. No booking, referral reward, settlement or property-approval lifecycle is altered by this migration.
