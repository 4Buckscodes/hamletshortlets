## 1. Executive identity

**Product:** HAMLET for Your Shortlets  
**Category:** Nigerian shortlet marketplace with accountability and partner-operating workflows.  
**Parent company:** Abundance Abodes.  
**Core USP:** Shortlets, with accountability.  
**Current customer-facing line:** Your stay, sorted.  
**Long-term ambition:** The operating system for African shortlets.

HAMLET connects guests looking for short-term accommodation with owners and authorised property partners. The product is designed to make discovery, booking requests, partner coordination, property information and transaction records more accountable.

HAMLET is not to be described as a property owner, hotel operator, legal title guarantor, insurer, escrow provider or payment institution unless the relevant legal and operational arrangements are separately established.

## 2. The problem

Shortlet transactions often depend on fragmented channels and personal trust: social media, WhatsApp, agents, spreadsheets, direct transfers and manual calendar updates. This creates avoidable uncertainty around whether a property is genuine, who has authority to represent it, whether dates are available, what the guest will pay, who is responsible during a stay and how money or issues are reconciled.

The original Business Bible describes pain points across owners, guests and operators. These remain hypotheses to validate continuously, not universal claims about every Nigerian operator.

## 3. Value proposition

### Guests
- Discover shortlets with clear property facts, house rules and disclosed charges.
- Submit a booking request and understand the approval/payment sequence.
- See payment and booking status based on system/provider evidence.
- Report a stay issue through a recorded support process.

### Owners
- Present a property through a structured listing and review process.
- Authorise specific partners to perform defined work.
- Receive visibility into booking requests, availability and recorded financial allocations.
- Maintain a clearer record of listing, booking and issue activity.

### Partners
- Join a network that recognises owners, authorised managers, operators, co-hosts, closing agents and referral partners.
- Access only properties and workflows for which they have permission.
- Receive transparent attribution and eligible earning records.
- Coordinate bookings and operational tasks within assigned authority.

### HAMLET
- Build a trusted local marketplace with repeatable operating records.
- Earn according to approved, disclosed commercial rules.
- Learn from completed bookings, quality supply, support outcomes and reconciled financial activity.

## 5. Users and ecosystem

- Guest / traveller.
- Property owner.
- Authorised property manager / co-host / shortlet operator.
- Closing agent / booking partner.
- Property introducer / network referrer.
- HAMLET operations and administrator.

A single person may hold multiple partner capabilities. The data model and user interface must keep each activity and permission distinct. Network membership is not the same as privileged partner approval or property authority.

## 6. Business model and commercial principles

The current repository-recorded commercial baseline is defined in [Financial Pricing & Ledger](05_FINANCIAL_PRICING_AND_LEDGER.md) and [Pricing & Payment Contract](../PRICING_AND_PAYMENT_CONTRACT.md).

Working baseline recorded in the 29 October 2026 repository specification:
- Retired the previous 3% guest-only platform fee.
- Domestic guest accommodation amount = owner-listed accommodation base B × 1.20.
- Owner fee deducted from B = 0%.
- The 20% pool is allocated between closing-agent commission, eligible direct-referrer reward, HAMLET allocation and internal payment/settlement reserve.
- International USD/GBP quotes include a separate 10% buffer applied once to qualifying stay amounts.
- Mandatory charges are itemised separately.
- Refundable caution deposits are separate liabilities and excluded from markup, FX buffer and reward allocations.
- Provider fee pass-through is not yet approved.

This is a repository-recorded policy baseline pending founder confirmation. It must not be expanded into claims about net profitability, tax liability or provider approval without evidence.

## 7. Product and operating principles

1. **Trust through evidence:** distinguish identity, authority, ownership evidence and inspection; never imply a title guarantee.
2. **One property identity:** avoid duplicate public listings; ambiguous matches require review.
3. **Permission before action:** owner-authorised scope controls what a partner can see or change.
4. **Request-to-book honesty:** a request is not an accepted booking; acceptance is not payment; payment is confirmed only through verified provider evidence.
5. **Transparent money:** itemise accommodation, mandatory charges, caution deposits, commercial allocations, provider fees, tax and refunds separately.
6. **Accountability:** material changes and decisions must have actors, timestamps and reasons.
7. **Local-first:** build around Nigerian shortlet realities, connectivity, payment methods, partner relationships and operational constraints.
8. **AI assists; verified facts decide:** AI may help search or prepare information but must not invent availability, verification or booking status.
9. **Privacy with accountability:** collect the minimum data needed and enforce role-based access.
10. **Quality over raw listing count:** only accurate, owner-authorised and genuinely bookable properties count as live supply.

## 9. Success measures

The current product specification emphasises completed bookings and reconciled transactions over vanity metrics. Recommended operating scorecard:
- approved, accurate, bookable properties;
- active properties with availability recently confirmed;
- qualified property views and booking requests;
- partner acceptance rate and time to response;
- payment success and payment-to-confirmation latency;
- completed bookings and repeat bookings;
- cancellation, refund and dispute rates;
- guest issue resolution time;
- owner/partner retention;
- gross booking value and net HAMLET revenue after actual costs;
- ledger reconciliation exceptions.

Do not report targets as actual performance. Define every metric, denominator, source table and reporting period before dashboards are trusted.

## 10. Strategic boundaries

HAMLET's long-term ambition is broader than its current MVP. The first product must prove one reliable loop: authorised property onboarding → accurate public listing → guest discovery → request review → payment verification → stay → issue resolution if needed → settlement and learning.

Do not expand to multiple cities, complex property-management automation, AI verification, cleaning marketplaces or financial custody until the core loop and required legal/provider permissions are proven.
