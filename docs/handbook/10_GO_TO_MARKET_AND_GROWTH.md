# HAMLET Go-to-Market & Growth Plan

**Version:** 2.0 draft · **Reviewed:** 2 November 2026  
**Status:** Rolling plan; October 2026 research/launch calendar is historical and must not be reported as achieved.

## 1. Market entry strategy

Launch transactionally in Lagos first. Build a quality supply base and reliable local operating relationships before expanding to Abuja or Ibadan. The 2026 December rush is a demand opportunity, not a reason to release unreliable payments, unverified listings or unsupported service promises.

## 2. Supply strategy

Recruit owners, developers, shortlet operators and agents through trusted introductions and direct outreach. Explain that HAMLET offers more than a listing: structured property presentation, owner-authorised partner workflows, booking visibility and accountability records.

Supply quality standard:
- owner/representative authority recorded;
- property facts and capacity confirmed;
- current authorised photos;
- amenities and house rules accurate;
- nightly price, mandatory charges and caution fee disclosed;
- calendar responsibility identified and availability process agreed;
- support/incident contact established;
- listing reviewed and approved before public booking.

Fictional showcase concepts are marketing prototypes only and do not count as supply or bookable inventory.

## 3. Partner acquisition

Make the partner network useful to owners, managers, operators and closing agents. Show partners their exact permissions, attribution, earning basis and status. Do not promise universal agent commissions until the owner-offer and central-pool relationship is resolved. Encourage referral only where the economics and fraud controls are clear.

## 4. Guest acquisition

Prioritise local search intent, property/area content, WhatsApp sharing, short-form video, founder-led education and referrals. Use actual property inventory and honest availability. If the live marketplace has no approved inventory, communicate early access or interest honestly; do not simulate availability or booking.

## 5. Positioning and content

Core USP: “Shortlets, with accountability.” Customer-facing line in current repository: “Your stay, sorted.” Campaign content should answer:
- where is the property and who is it suitable for?
- what is genuinely included?
- what is the full amount due?
- what happens after a booking request?
- what evidence does HAMLET check?
- who supports the guest and owner?

Avoid generic luxury claims and unverifiable “verified” badges.

## 6. Pilot design

Run a controlled Lagos pilot with a limited number of owner-authorised, approved and actively bookable properties. Measure actual completed stays and reconcile every transaction. Recruit enough owner/partner coverage to respond to booking requests and guest issues.

## 7. Metrics

Track:
- owner/partner leads → qualified → onboarded → approved listings;
- active approved properties and availability freshness;
- guest visits → property views → booking requests → partner acceptance → payment → completed stay;
- conversion and drop-off at each stage;
- response times and failed/expired requests;
- payment success and webhook exceptions;
- cancellations, refunds, disputes and incidents;
- completed bookings, repeat booking and referrals;
- owner/partner retention;
- gross booking value, platform allocation and actual contribution after provider/support costs.

Define source tables, attribution, denominator and reporting cadence before publishing KPI results.

## 8. Expansion gates

Do not open Abuja/Ibadan transactional booking until Lagos has:
- adequate verified, genuinely bookable supply;
- repeatable owner/partner onboarding;
- working support and incident escalation;
- successful payment/refund tests;
- reconciled owner/partner/HAMLET/deposit ledgers;
- evidence of guest conversion and retention;
- jurisdiction-specific tax/legal review.

## 9. Historical planning material

The original October/November/December 2026 plan remains useful for interview questions, founder-led outreach, content themes and supply acquisition ideas. Its dates, targets and claims must be updated with actual results. Record completed interviews, partner commitments, real listings and completed bookings as evidence rather than copying targets into performance reporting.
