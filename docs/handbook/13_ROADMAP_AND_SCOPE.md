# HAMLET Roadmap & Scope

**Version:** 2.0 draft · **Reviewed:** 2 November 2026  
**Status:** Scope proposal aligned to current MVP specification; prioritisation requires founder approval.

## Release 1 — Lagos transactional MVP

- Guest discovery and property detail.
- Guest account and booking request.
- Owner/partner network and permission-controlled property submission.
- Property evidence review, duplicate-risk review and publication.
- Availability and booking lifecycle.
- Transparent pricing and caution-deposit disclosure.
- Provider checkout and verified payment confirmation.
- Guest booking/issue workspace.
- Partner property, booking, availability and earnings workspace.
- Admin property/partner/booking/issue/settlement operations.
- Notifications, audit logs and reconciliation.
- Controlled pilot with real approved inventory.

## Release 1 hardening / launch dependencies

- Verify pricing allocation snapshot and ledger flow end-to-end.
- Resolve owner-defined fixed/net-rate deal model versus 10% closing-agent allocation.
- Approve provider-fee handling and provider marketplace/fund-flow permissions.
- Approve tax classification, tax collection and remittance responsibilities.
- Approve cancellation, early checkout, no-show and refund matrix.
- Approve caution-deposit custody, evidence and refund process.
- Verify RLS, security-definer RPCs and storage permissions.
- Complete real-inventory and operational support readiness.

## Later / post-MVP candidates

- Public reviews and richer reputation.
- Horatio conversational discovery/assistant after verified inventory/search quality.
- Owner command centre and deeper property operating reports.
- Advanced calendar integrations and channel management.
- Dynamic pricing and occupancy analytics.
- Cleaning, maintenance and vendor coordination.
- Advanced fraud/document automation with human oversight.
- More currencies and payment providers.
- Abuja and Ibadan market expansion.
- Education/owner academy and promotional marketplace tools.

## Explicit non-goals until approved

- Instant booking.
- Guaranteed property title/ownership.
- Insurance or damage guarantee.
- Escrow, wallet or custody of third-party funds.
- Automated tax/legal advice.
- Fake inventory or fabricated availability.
- Public reviews before moderation and operational processes exist.
- Expansion purely to increase geographic coverage without local operations.

## Prioritisation rule

Prioritise work that improves trust, successful completed bookings, accurate financial settlement, partner accountability, guest safety and owner retention. Do not add features solely because they are technically interesting or present in the long-term brand vision.
